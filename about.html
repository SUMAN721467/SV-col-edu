About Our Institution

A heritage of character building, holistic teacher education, and pedagogical devotion in rural Bengal.

Genesis & Inspiration

Sahid Khudiram Memorial Trust

Swami Vivekananda College of Education (SVCE) was founded with a profound commitment to uplifting rural and semi-urban education across Paschim Medinipur and West Bengal. Established and managed by the esteemed Sahid Khudiram Memorial Trust, the institution embodies patriotic dedication and self-reliant character building.

Recognized by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) and affiliated with the West Bengal Board of Primary Education (WBBPE) and Baba Saheb Ambedkar Education University (BSAEU / WBUTTEPA), SVCE serves as a beacon for aspiring educators.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To develop into a center of excellence in teacher education that combines moral values, scientific pedagogical inquiry, and modern instructional technology to cultivate nation-building teachers.

Our Mission

  • Provide experiential learning with smart classrooms and laboratories.
  • Instill high ethical standards, empathy, and social responsibility.
  • Offer inclusive, accessible training to meritorious candidates from diverse socio-economic backgrounds.
