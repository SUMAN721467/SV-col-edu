NCTE Recognition Order: D.El.Ed Course (Intake: 50)

To be published in Gazette of India Part - III, Section 4

Order No: ERC/7-162.6.8/NCTE/D.El.Ed./2013/21191 • 15/10/2013

The Eastern Regional Committee (NCTE) in exercise of the powers vested under Section 14(3)(a) of the NCTE Act 1993, granted recognition to Swami Vivekananda College of Education for conducting D.El.Ed. Course of Elementary Level of two year duration with an annual intake of 50 (fifty) students from academic session 2014-2015.

  • Strict adherence to norms & standards prescribed in NCTE Regulations 2009 / 2014.
  • Admissions subject to formal affiliation with examining body.
  • Required number of academic staff for conducting course always kept in position.
  • Submission of annual audited self-appraisal report.
