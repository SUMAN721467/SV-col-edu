Programs of Study

Comprehensive teacher education pathways recognized by NCTE and affiliated with apex regulatory councils.

ERCAPP77 / 2012

Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed)

Elementary Level Teacher Training Program • 2-Year Full-Time Regular Course

Annual Intake 50 Seats
Affiliating Body WBBPE, Kolkata
Order Ref ERC/7-162.6.8/2013
Medium of Instruction Bengali & English

Eligibility & Admission Norms

  • Candidates must have passed Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent examination from a recognized Board/Council with minimum 50% marks in aggregate.
  • 5% relaxation in marks (45%) is admissible for reserved category candidates (SC, ST, OBC-A, OBC-B, and PH).
  • Admission is processed strictly through the West Bengal Board of Primary Education (WBBPE) centralised online portal.

Curricular Framework

  • Core Papers: Child Studies, Educational Philosophy, Contemporary Indian Society & Language Education.
  • Pedagogy of Subjects: Bengali, English, Mathematics, and Environmental Studies (EVS).
  • Practicum: ICT Application in Primary Classrooms, School Internship, Drama & Art in Education.
ERCAPP3967

Bachelor of Education (B.Ed)

Secondary & Higher Secondary Teacher Preparation Program • 2-Year Full-Time Regular Course

Annual Intake 50 Seats (1 Unit)
Affiliating University BSAEU / Vidyasagar Univ
Order Ref APP3967/B.Ed./2016
Category Self-Financed

Eligibility & Method Subjects

Candidates with at least 50% marks in Bachelor's Degree and/or Master's Degree in Sciences / Social Sciences / Humanities. 55% for Bachelor's in Engineering/Technology. 5% relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PWD.

Method Subjects Offered at SVCE:
Bengali, English, History, Geography, Life Science, Mathematics, Computer Science, and Music.

School Internship & Practical Training

  • Micro-Teaching Laboratory: Skill practice in stimulus variation, blackboard writing, questioning, and reinforcement.
  • 16-Week School Internship: Real classroom engagement across affiliated partner secondary schools in Kharagpur and Keshiary.
  • EPC Courses: Reading & Reflecting on Texts, Drama & Art in Education, Critical Understanding of ICT, and Yoga Education.
